I am an astronomer (*Astronome-adjoint*) at the **Laboratoire d'Astrophysique de Marseille (LAM)**, based since 2023 at the **Observatoire de Haute-Provence (OHP)**, home of the 1.93 m telescope where the first exoplanet around a Sun-like star, 51 Peg b, was discovered in 1995.

My research focuses on **finding and characterising exoplanets with high-precision radial velocities**, and on understanding the instrumental and stellar "noise" that limits these measurements. I lead high-precision exoplanet programmes on the **SOPHIE** spectrograph, work with the near-infrared spectropolarimeter **SPIRou** at CFHT, and am deeply involved in instrumentation:

- **PI and project lead of SOPHIE-red**, the upgrade that extends SOPHIE's wavelength coverage towards the near-infrared and keeps the instrument at the forefront beyond 2030;
- **French representative on the Executive Board of ANDES**, the high-resolution spectrograph for ESO's Extremely Large Telescope.

## Research interests

- **Exoplanet detection and characterisation**: low-mass planets around bright G, K and M dwarfs, long-period planets, multi-planet system architectures, and circumbinary planets
- **Stellar activity** (chromospheric and photospheric) in the optical and near-infrared: observations, simulations and correction methods for radial velocities
- **High-resolution spectroscopy**: observations, instrumentation, calibration and data-reduction pipelines

## Highlights

- Discovery with SOPHIE of **Gl 411 b**, a super-Earth only 8 light-years away ([press release](https://www.unige.ch/sciences/astro/news/gliese411b))
- **HD 88986**: a system including a cool sub-Neptune potentially transiting a naked-eye star (V = 6.5) ([press release](https://www.insu.cnrs.fr/fr/cnrsinfo/decouverte-dun-systeme-extrasolaire-comprenant-une-sous-neptune-froide))
- **Circumbinary ("Tatooine") planets** detected from the ground with radial velocities, within the BEBOP project ([press release](https://www.insu.cnrs.fr/fr/cnrsinfo/une-exoplanete-de-type-tatooine-reperee-par-un-telescope-terrestre))
- Contribution to the detection of a **six-planet system in resonance** ([press release](https://www.insu.cnrs.fr/fr/cnrsinfo/detection-dun-systeme-de-six-planetes-en-resonance))
