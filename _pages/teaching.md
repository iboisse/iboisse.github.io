## Teaching

I teach around 66 hours per year at **Aix-Marseille Université (AMU)**:

- **Solid mechanics** (Bachelor, L2), distance learning: lectures and tutorials, including a 70-page course and exercise book
- **Collisions and celestial mechanics** (Bachelor, L3), distance learning: lectures and tutorials, including a 60-page course and exercise book
- **Introduction to astronomical observations** (Master): tutorials and practical work
- **Doppler spectroscopy** (Master): lecture and practical work
- **Student research projects** (L2 to M1) at OHP and LAM
- **Exoplanet lectures** for the Université du Temps Libre (AMU lifelong-learning programme) in Marseille and Aix-en-Provence

Previously: practical training on the SOPHIE detector at the **FOCUS school** at OHP (Université Grenoble-Alpes, master and PhD level), physics tutorials and lab work at Université Pierre et Marie Curie, computer science (L1) and optics lab work (IUT).

## PhD supervision

- **Loula Grenier-Frassati** (2025 – 2028, co-supervised with J.-L. Beuzit): *Extending high-precision radial velocities toward the near-infrared for SOPHIE-red*
- **Pia Cortes-Zuleta** (2019 – 2023): *Optimization of the SPIRou data processing: improving exoplanet detection and characterization around low-mass stars*. Now a postdoc at the University of St Andrews.
- **Neda Heidari** (2019 – 2022): *Overcoming the stellar activity impact on exoplanet detection and characterization: application to the follow-up of TESS candidates with SOPHIE*. Then a CNES postdoctoral fellow at IAP.
- **Melissa Hobson** (2016 – 2019, co-supervised with F. Bouchy): *Exoplanet detection around M dwarfs with near-infrared and visible spectroscopy*

I have also supervised around 20 Bachelor and Master interns since 2014, on topics ranging from radial-velocity analysis to detector characterisation for SOPHIE-red.

## Outreach

- **Space Guardians**: scientific lead of two successive Erasmus+ projects (2017 – 2023) developing free educational material to introduce science through astronomy to children aged 3 to 10 ([presentation](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2022sf2a.conf..121B/abstract))
- Regular visits to nursery and primary school classes, notably in rural areas of the Alpes-de-Haute-Provence and Hautes-Alpes, through *Science dans les classes* and *Les Savanturiers*
- Fête de la Science and Festival d'Astronomie en Provence every year
- Public talks, including a joint lecture with Michel Mayor in 2025 on 30 years of exoplanet discoveries
- Articles for *The Conversation* and *Le Petit Quotidien*, and interviews for radio, press and TV (France Culture, ARTE)
- AstroGirls days at LAM, and a documentary on women in science
